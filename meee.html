The Story of My Name

Family Tradition

Originally planned

My second name was supposed to be Vincent, following a tradition from my grandfather Vicente.

A Change for Uniqueness

A personal decision

Because many Vincents were in our family, my parents chose something distinct.

Faith and Place

Anthony was chosen in honor of St. Anthony de Padua, a saint celebrated in our hometown.

How My Name Reflects Me

My name represents a balance between individuality and tradition. My first name reflects independence, while my second name connects me to faith, culture, and community.

d) How do you feel about your name?

Would I Change My Name?

No. My name already tells my story. It reminds me where I came from and who I am becoming.

PSY 100 – Understanding the Self | Activity 2

  • a) What is your full name?
    Ryle Anthony Gabotero
  • b) What is the meaning or origin of your name?
    Ryle reflects strength and individuality. Anthony comes from the Roman name Antonius, often interpreted as priceless or worthy of value.
  • c) Who gave you your name and why?
    My parents gave me my name. Originally it was supposed to be Vincent (from my grandfather Vicente), but they chose Anthony to honor St. Anthony de Padua and for uniqueness.
  • d) How do you feel about your name?
    I feel proud and connected to my family, culture, and faith.
  • e) In what ways does your name reflect your personality or identity?
    My first name shows individuality; my second name connects me to faith, culture, and tradition.
  • f) If you could change your name, would you? Why or why not?
    No. My name tells my story and reminds me where I came from and who I am becoming.
